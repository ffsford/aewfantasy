Trades

You may trade at any point (except when a show is live). Both rosters must end at 8 wrestlers after the trade (or 9 if a player has the Foreign Object roster expansion). Trades are locked in the last 30 days of the season and can be subject to veto.

Foreign Objects

After every AEW special TV event or PPV a Foreign Object can be awarded. Effects include the Wheel of Boom and other one-time advantages.

Tag Team Bonus Points (applied to player)

  • Having 2 members drafted for a tag team match +2
  • Having 3 members drafted for a tag team match +3
  • Having 4 members drafted for a tag team match +4
  • Having 5 members drafted for a tag team match +5

Rivalry Bonus Points (applied to player)

  • Having 2 members drafted as opponents +2
  • Having 3 members drafted as opponents +3
  • Having 4 members drafted as opponents +4
  • Having 5 members drafted as opponents +5

Ending Multi-Person Match (applied to wrestler)

  • Ending a multi-person match +1

Tournament Bonus Points (applied to wrestler)

  • Winning opening round match +1
  • Winning additional match(es) +2
  • Winning tournament finals +3
